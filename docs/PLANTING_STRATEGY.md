# Planting & biodiversity strategy

Climate: humid continental, Podlasie — roughly USDA zone 5b–6a (winter lows to about −28 °C). All species below are frost-hardy there. Soil, pH and groundwater are **not known**; nothing is assumed wet, dry or shaded. Soil survey and a percolation test come first.

## Principles
1. **Layered, native-led:** canopy → sub-canopy → shrub → herb → moss. Native species dominate the woody structure; non-native cultivars are limited to fruit.
2. **No empty lawn.** Retained pasture is mown paths only; everything else is meadow, woodland, hedge or productive garden.
3. **Low-impact construction:** screw piles under pavilions and boardwalk; no strip foundations; permeable surfaces; paving only at the entrance apron (≈ 50 m², plan allows 98 m²).
4. **Sun first for food:** no trees are generated within ≈ 8 m south of the greenhouse; woodland is concentrated north and west where it shelters and screens.

## Zones

| Zone | Composition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Native woodland | Scots pine, silver birch, rowan, aspen, bird cherry, a few spruce/alder; hazel, juniper, guelder rose, dog rose, hawthorn; bilberry/lingonberry and ferns | Privacy, habitat, forest-bathing atmosphere |
| Hedgerow / screen | Hazel, hawthorn, guelder rose, dog rose, juniper, elder with birch and rowan | Road and neighbour screening, nesting cover |
| Wildflower meadow | Ox-eye daisy, yarrow, cornflower, knapweed, field scabious, bird's-foot trefoil, meadow sage, red clover, wild carrot (local-ecotype seed); two cuts a year | Pollinators, seasonal colour |
| Food forest | Antonówka apple, Węgierka plum, Faworytka pear, sour cherry, rowan; currants, gooseberry, honeyberry, hazel, raspberry | Fruit, blossom, bird food |
| Soft fruit / kitchen garden | Berry rows, herbs, raised no-dig beds, greenhouse | Daily food close to the house and compost |
| Shade garden | Ferns, bilberry, moss under rowan and birch | Quiet woodland-floor planting near the meditation corner |
| Wetland margin | Alder, guelder rose, sedges/iris around the rain garden and pond | Water, amphibians, dragonflies |

## Seasonal sequence
Hazel catkins (Feb–Mar) → apple/plum blossom and bird cherry (Apr–May) → ox-eye daisy, bird's-foot trefoil, meadow sage (May–Jun) → cornflower, knapweed, scabious, clover, yarrow (Jun–Sep) → rowan berries and autumn colour (Sep–Oct) → seed heads and snow-laden pine in winter. The Strategy panel in the app charts this.

## Habitat features
Log piles and standing deadwood snags, insect hotel, bird boxes on poles (design allowance ≈ 6), hibernaculum stone pile, wildlife pond (no fish), 13 cm gaps under timber fence screens, thorny hedges, uncut meadow margins over winter, shielded 2200 K path lights switched off late evening.

## Water
Roof water → rain tanks (house) and greenhouse barrel; overflow → rain garden → pond/wet meadow in the south-east, the lowest part of the plot. Permeable boardwalk and stepping stones; no gravel fields. Outdoor shower / bania drainage must go to a permitted infiltration or sewer connection — to verify.

## Soil
No-dig beds, wood-chip and leaf-mould mulch, on-site compost, minimal disturbance. The plan's soil class (brown soil, pasture V) suggests moderately fertile mineral soil; acid-loving plants (bilberry, blueberry) need a pH test.

## Care calendar
* **Mar–Apr:** prune fruit trees, coppice hazel, patch-sow meadow.
* **Jun & Sep:** cut meadow, remove cuttings.
* **Autumn:** leave leaf litter under shrubs, mulch beds, plant bare-root trees.
* **Winter:** keep seed heads; clear snow only from boardwalk and gates.
